## Description — EN

```
Line Browser is a fast, private browser with a built-in ad and tracker blocker, a secure password vault, and network tools that show you exactly what your device is doing.

BLOCK ADS & TRACKERS
Block ads, trackers, and unwanted domains across every site you visit. The list runs entirely on your device — nothing is sent to any server.

PASSWORDS & VAULT
Save logins in the iOS Keychain and reveal, copy, or delete them in one tap. Your private vault is encrypted on-device with AES-256-GCM and opens only with Face ID or your passcode.

NETWORK MONITOR
Watch every request each page makes — images, scripts, fonts, analytics — with status codes and sizes. Find out which sites phone home.

WEB APPS & TABS
Install sites as web apps, keep them one tap away, and manage every open tab from a single overview.

PRIVACY FIRST
No accounts. No analytics. No trackers. No servers. Your history, passwords, and logs never leave your device.

BEAUTIFUL & BILINGUAL
Dark and light themes, custom wallpapers, and a full interface in English and Romanian — switch instantly in Settings.

Your browser, your rules. Line Browser.
```
